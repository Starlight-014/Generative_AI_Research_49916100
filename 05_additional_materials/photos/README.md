# Photographs — mixed sensitivity

Photographs **of people** (workshop sessions, participants at the focus group) are
identifiable and are stored in UQ RDM, collection `06_media_identifiable`. They are
excluded from Git by the image-extension rules in `.gitignore`.

Photographs of **materials only** (whiteboard affinity diagrams with no faces or
handwriting attributable to an individual) may be committed here once reviewed, and
must be named under the project convention, e.g.
`GENAI_affinity-diagram_20260728_v01.png`.

The distinction is made by the chief investigator before any image is added, and the
decision is recorded in `00_admin/project_management/`. Where a photograph of
materials is the only record of an analysis step, prefer transcribing it into a
text or Markdown file — text diffs, images do not.

# Consent form (blank template, version 02)

**Study:** Using Generative AI Tools: Boon or Bane? · HREC 2026/GENAI/0417

This blank template is committed because it documents what participants agreed to,
which a reader needs in order to judge what the data may be used for. **Signed copies
are never committed** -- see `../../03_qualitative/consent/README.md`.

Please initial each box you agree to.

*Note for the team: the last two boxes are separate deliberately. A participant may
consent to being quoted in our paper without consenting to their transcript being
released as a dataset, and 4 of 18 participants made exactly that distinction. The
embargo decision recorded as an open item in the data management plan applies only to
those who ticked the final box.*

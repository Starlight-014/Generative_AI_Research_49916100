# Research proposal (version 04 -- submitted)

**Using Generative AI Tools: Boon or Bane?**
Submitted to the school research committee 20 May 2026 · Approved 3 June 2026
HREC application 2026/GENAI/0417 lodged 22 May 2026, approved 14 June 2026

## Aim

To characterise how students and academic staff at one Australian university perceive
the benefits and risks of generative AI tools, and to identify what distinguishes
those who regard them as net beneficial from those who do not.

## Design

Explanatory sequential mixed methods. A cross-sectional survey (target n = 60)
establishes the distribution of use, perceived benefit, perceived risk and disclosure
behaviour; semi-structured interviews (target n = 18, sampled from survey
respondents) then explain the patterns the survey cannot. The survey instrument is
administered unchanged to students and staff, which addresses the gap identified in
the literature review: existing studies survey the two groups with different
instruments and then compare the results.

## Research questions

- RQ1. How frequently, and for what tasks, are generative AI tools used?
- RQ2. What benefits and risks do users perceive, and how are the two related?
- RQ3. Do students and staff differ, and on what?
- RQ4. What do users actually do about disclosure, and what explains it?

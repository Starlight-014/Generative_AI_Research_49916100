# Semi-structured interview protocol (version 02)

Project `GENAI` · HREC 2026/GENAI/0417 · 18 interviews conducted 1-28 July 2026
Duration 35-50 minutes · Audio recorded with consent · Conducted in person or via Zoom

## Before starting

1. Confirm the participant has read the information sheet and ask if they have
   questions.
2. Confirm consent to record. **If consent to record is withheld, proceed with
   written notes only** and mark the transcript accordingly.
3. Remind the participant they may skip any question or stop at any time, and that
   nothing they say will be attributed to them by name.
4. Start the recorder and state the participant code and date only -- never the name.

## Opening

- Tell me a bit about your studies or your role here.
- When did you first use a generative AI tool for university work?

## Section 1. Practice

- Walk me through the last time you used one of these tools for university work.
  What were you trying to do?
- *(probe)* What did you do with what it produced?
- *(probe)* How did you decide whether to trust it?
- Are there tasks you deliberately do not use them for? What makes those different?

## Section 2. Benefit

- What do these tools actually do well, in your experience?
- Has using them changed how you work, beyond saving time?
- *(probe)* Has anything you learned to do yourself become harder, or easier?

## Section 3. Concern

- What worries you, if anything, about using these tools?
- *(probe, added in v02)* Several survey respondents rated both the benefits and the
  risks highly at the same time. Does that match your experience? How do you hold
  both of those at once?
- What would have to change for you to worry less?

## Section 4. Disclosure and rules

- What are you supposed to do about declaring AI use in your work?
- *(probe)* Is that clear? Where did you learn it?
- Have you ever been unsure whether something counted as needing to be declared?

## Section 5. Closing

- If the university could do one thing about generative AI, what should it be?
- Is there anything I should have asked you about and did not?

## After the interview

- Stop the recording before any informal conversation resumes.
- Upload audio to the RDM collection `05_recordings` the same day; delete from the
  recorder once the upload is verified.
- Write a one-paragraph contact summary within 24 hours while recall is fresh.

## Changes from version 01

The v02 probe in Section 3 was added on 18 June 2026 after the first six interviews,
in response to the survey finding that perceived benefit and concern are statistically
independent. Interviews P01-P06 did not receive this probe, which is recorded in the
coding matrix so that its absence is not mistaken for a participant declining to
answer.

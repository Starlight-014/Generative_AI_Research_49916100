# Qualitative codebook (version 03)

Project `GENAI` · 18 interviews · Coded in NVivo, exported to Markdown for version control
Version 03 incorporates the changes agreed at the 8 August coding meeting.

Two coders coded all 18 transcripts independently. Agreement on the first six
transcripts was 0.74 (Cohen kappa) before the v02 revisions and 0.86 after; the
remaining twelve were coded at 0.86-0.91. Disagreements were resolved by discussion,
not by a third coder, and the resolutions are in
`GENAI_coding-disagreements_20260810_v01.md` in RDM.

---

## Family A. Practice

**A1 Task substitution** -- the tool performs a task the participant would otherwise
have done themselves and the output is used.
*Include:* drafting, generating variations, producing boilerplate.
*Exclude:* cases where the output is discarded (see A2).
*Example:* P03 on generating practice questions.

**A2 Output as mirror** -- the tool's output is not used, but the participant's
reaction to it is. Added in v02 after this pattern appeared in four transcripts and
did not fit A1.
*Example:* P01, "I almost never use the text. I use the reaction."

**A3 Verification practice** -- what the participant does to check the output.
*Sub-codes:* A3a executes/tests it; A3b checks against an independent source;
A3c checks against own knowledge; A3d does not check.

**A4 Abstention** -- tasks the participant deliberately does not use the tool for,
and the stated reason.

## Family B. Benefit

**B1 Time and volume** -- speed, throughput, producing many options.
**B2 Access to a responsive other** -- the tool substitutes for a person who is
unavailable. Distinguished from B1 in v03 because availability, not speed, is what
participants describe.
*Example:* P01 on supervisor availability; P02 on "being available at 1am".
**B3 Comprehension support** -- explaining, translating, restating.

## Family C. Concern

**C1 Factual unreliability** -- fabrication, confident error, invented citations.
**C2 Integrity and assessment validity** -- misconduct, assessment no longer
measuring what it claims to.
**C3 Erosion of own capability** -- the participant reports a skill of their own
weakening.
**C4 Privacy and confidentiality** -- putting third-party data into the system.
Added in v03; previously coded under C2, which conflated a rule about honesty with a
rule about data protection.
**C5 Dependence and continuity** -- reliance on something that may become
unavailable, paywalled or changed.
**C6 Opacity of others' use** -- inability to tell whether a colleague's or a
student's work is their own.

## Family D. Governance

**D1 Rule clarity** -- whether the participant knows what is required.
**D2 Rule inconsistency** -- rules differing between courses, schools or publishers.
**D3 Disclosure practice** -- what the participant actually does.
**D4 Requested institutional action.**

---

## Coding rules

1. **Code to the smallest meaningful unit**, usually one to three sentences, not the
   whole turn.
2. **Double-code freely across families**, but not within a family. A passage may be
   both B2 and C3; it may not be both C1 and C2 (choose the dominant concern, and
   record the decision if it was close).
3. **Do not code the interviewer's questions**, including where the question contains
   the construct.
4. **An absent code is not a negative finding.** P01-P06 were not asked the v02 probe;
   the absence of C-family codes in that section reflects the protocol, not the
   participant.
